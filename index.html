Trusses, Feb 2027 rig

Positions come from the top-down rigging sketch, with everything upstage of the beam moved 1′ downstage on 17 Oct. The 70′ Screen 1 truss rides just above the screen and moves with Screen 1’s sliders. The others open at the trims set on 15 Oct. Each slider sets one pick point’s trim, the bottom of the truss above the deck. Uneven points tilt the truss without stretching it, so its ends swing in and the chains lean slightly; two points can differ by no more than their spacing along the truss. Green dots are the 600 lb hang points.
